# Projector & Audio Story Box Minigame Atlas

A bilingual decision atlas connecting 50 public-domain stories with Projector and Audio Story Box minigame concepts.

The published site includes:

- 50 classic story profiles, sorted by decision score.
- 24 stories with two embedded minigames each.
- 10 detailed story blueprints.
- 30 reusable story-interaction mechanics and 20 standalone Free Play games.
- Bidirectional story/game links, bilingual details, filters, and same-type comparison.

## Published site

https://wisdomoasis.github.io/storybox-minigame-atlas/

## Source files

- `assets/story-data.js`: bilingual story profiles and story-game relationships.
- `assets/game-data.js`: the existing 30 interaction and 20 standalone game concepts.
- `scripts/generate-story-markdown.js`: emits the matching Obsidian review document.
